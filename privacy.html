PadProxy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Summary

PadProxy does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Network traffic

PadProxy runs a proxy server on your own iPhone or iPad. Devices you configure on your local network send web requests to it, and PadProxy forwards them to their destination over your device's internet connection. Encrypted (HTTPS) traffic is relayed as-is and is never decrypted. Nothing is logged to disk or sent to the developer; the connection list shown in the app lives only in memory and is cleared when the app closes.

Local network access

iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network. This is only used so your other devices can connect to the proxy.

Tips

Optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details or personal information.

Settings

Your chosen port and preferences are stored on your device only.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/nirasnet/padproxy-site.
